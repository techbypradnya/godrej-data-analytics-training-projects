All projects are built on a **Financial Sales Dataset** containing real-world business transactions.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|

| Country | Country of transaction |

| Sales | Total sales value |

## 🚀 Projects

### Project 1 — Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

> **Objective:** Analyze overall sales and profit performance across products and customer segments.

**Key Insights:**
- 🏆 **Government** segment contributes the highest share of overall profit
- 📦 **Paseo** achieved the highest sales and profit among all products
- 💡 Balanced discounts improved profitability across the product range

**Visualizations:** Donut Chart · Area Chart · Scatter Plot · Waterfall Chart

---

### Project 2 — Discount & Profitability Analysis

> **Objective:** Understand how discount bands influence profitability across countries and business segments.

**Key Insights:**
- 💰 **Low and Medium discounts** generate better profits across countries
- 🏛️ **Government** and **Midmarket** segments are the strongest contributors
- ⚠️ High discounts significantly reduce profitability, especially for Channel Partners

**Visualizations:** Multi-Line Charts · Stacked Column Charts · Profit Comparison Dashboards

---

### Project 3 — Country-wise Performance Analysis

> **Objective:** Analyze sales and profit distribution across countries.

**Key Insights:**
- 🥇 **France** achieved the highest profit share
- 🌱 **USA** and **Mexico** show strong potential for future business growth
- 🌍 **Germany** and **Canada** also contributed significantly to overall profitability

**Visualizations:** Pie Charts · Profit Distribution Charts · Country Comparison Dashboards

---

### Project 4 — Customer Segment Analysis

> **Objective:** Understand how customer segments contribute to overall sales and profitability.

**Key Insights:**
- 🏛️ **Government** segment generated the highest sales and profit across all products
- 📉 **Enterprise** and **Channel Partners** contributed comparatively lower profits
- 📊 Segment analysis reveals clear opportunities for targeted growth strategies

**Visualizations:** Stacked Column Charts · Segment Comparison Dashboards
